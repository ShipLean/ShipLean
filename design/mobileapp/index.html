Phone app: first mock-ups

The native app (epic SHIP-905) does less than the web app and works by voice: notes first, then keeping an eye on progress, answering what people and agents wait on you for, and changing priority. Tasks only show as totals, and boards are read-only. Four areas, 35 states, light and dark, at 390 × 844. Throwaway HTML on a small kit (app.css, app.js) over ../tokens.css; not the Expo app.

Every voice command

  1. Hold to talk, no wake word: the mic is in the tab bar on list screens and in a dock on detail screens.
  2. Listening: the screen's context, the live transcript, keys, names and states matched as hotwords.
  3. Preview: each change listed and removable; notes quoted word for word. Nothing changes until Apply or “yes”; Fix says it again.
  4. Undo toast after applying.

Shared decisions (Joel, 8 Nov 2026)

  • S1 "Feature board" in the app is progress across all Features; one Feature's own board is a read-only drill-in.
  • S2 A fifth tab, Inbox, holds everything waiting on you: Inbox · Backlog · [hold to talk] · Features · Retro. Not mocked up yet.
  • S3 A voice note keeps its audio clip next to the transcript for 30 days, playable by project members.
  • S4 Answers to spoken questions show on screen (a sheet when short, a screen when long), with Read aloud by on-device text to speech.
  • S5 Health (On track / At risk / Blocked) is a new Feature field, set by people and agents, shown on the web too.
  • S6 Needs you on a Feature counts the questions and decisions on its stories, everywhere.
  • S7 Native sizes: 44px tap targets and 15px body text. SHIP-805 stays the rule for the mobile web.
  • Agents never wait for an answer: anything asked of a person is answered on the work item, for the next agent that picks it up.

Work item 9 states · work-item.html · notes in work-item.md

A Feature or Story on the phone: status first, then what waits on you, then notes. Most commands leave a note.

Decisions (Joel, 8 Nov 2026)

  1. D1 Should Needs you come straight after the status line on a Feature? Recommended: yes, so what waits on you is the first card, with a compact progress line in the header.
  2. D2 Should the phone offer Still true on the status line? Recommended: yes, as a link and as the voice command "still true".
  3. D3 Should notes and changes share one timeline? Recommended: yes, with All / Notes / Changes filters.
  4. D4 Should a voice note keep its audio clip? Recommended: yes, the transcript first and the clip under it for checking what was said.
  5. D5 Can one voice command make several changes at once? Recommended: yes, previewed as separate rows that can each be removed.
  6. D6 Does approving a decision by voice need the preview like every other command? Recommended: yes, the same preview, Apply or "yes", then Undo.
  7. D7 Should replying to an agent's question by voice also hand the item back to the agent? Recommended, clarified: agents don't stay running to wait; the answer is posted on the work item for the next agent that picks it up.
  8. D8 Should a Story show its tasks as counts only, naming just the blocked ones? Recommended: yes.
  9. D9 Should answers to questions appear on screen, with Read aloud as an option? Recommended: yes, on screen first (this is SHIP-906 Q7).
  10. D10 While the models download, should the "Or say …" hints be hidden? Recommended: hide them and grey out the mic, with a banner showing progress.

Backlog 9 states · backlog.html · notes in backlog.md

Priority: rank order of Epics, Features and Stories, changed by voice ("move SHIP-906 above SHIP-907") or a Move sheet.

Decisions (Joel, 8 Nov 2026)

  1. D1 Should the level control offer Epics, Features and Stories? Recommended: all three, since the app is about those three levels.
  2. D2 Should the rank preview list every row whose position changes? Recommended: yes, as before → after with the moved row first.
  3. D3 Should a move that puts an item above the item it is blocked by be allowed? Recommended: allow it with a warning in the preview.
  4. D4 Is a Move sheet (Top, Up one, Down one, Bottom, Above or below…) the right touch fallback? Recommended: yes, no drag on the app.
  5. D5 When a spoken name matches several items, should the phone ask? Recommended: ask, with numbered choices to tap or say.
  6. D6 Should Needs me on the Features level include questions on their Stories? Recommended: yes, rolled up to the Feature.
  7. D7 Where does a Story captured by voice land when no position is said? Alternative: a Story captured by voice lands at the top of the backlog, so it gets looked at.
  8. D8 Should rank numbers show on every row? Recommended: yes, they make voice moves ("to 2") and the preview easy to follow.

Open questions

  • Should one command move several items ("put SHIP-906 and SHIP-892 at the top")?
  • Should the Stories level group by sprint, or stay one rank-ordered list?
  • Does rank have to be within one Epic, or across the whole project as on the web?
  • Who can reorder from the phone: any project member, or only the product owner?
  • How should the Epics level look: the same rows with Feature counts, or something closer to a roadmap?

Feature board 9 states · feature-board.html · notes in feature-board.md

Progress across Features: health, the status line and how fresh it is, progress, and what needs you.

Decisions (not answered yet)

  1. D1 Should the Features tab be a stacked list grouped by state? Recommended: yes, a stacked grouped list, because one scroll covers everything and it reads well with voice answers. Alternative: a sideways pager with one state per page, like the mobile web boards (SHIP-719). open
  2. D2 Should health (On track / At risk / Blocked) be set explicitly by people and agents? Recommended: yes, set by hand, by voice or by an agent via MCP. Alternative: compute it from stale status lines, blocked stories and open asks. open
  3. D3 Should the status line be a field of its own, changed only by "status: …"? Recommended: yes, so notes never overwrite it. Alternative: the latest note is the status line. open
  4. D4 When a status update says "blocked", should the preview also propose the health change as a separate line you can remove? Recommended: yes. Alternative: never infer health from words. open
  5. D5 Should a status line be flagged as stale after 3 days? Recommended: 3 days, fixed. Alternative: a per-project setting. open
  6. D6 Should groups run Active, Resolved, New, then Closed folded to one line? Recommended: yes, because what moves comes first. Alternative: the board's column order (New first). open
  7. D7 Can a Feature's state be changed in the app, by voice or a Change state sheet, without drag? Recommended: yes. Alternative: Features are read-only in the app too. open
  8. D8 When a Feature with open stories is moved to Closed, should the preview warn ("3 stories still open") and still allow Apply? Recommended: warn and allow, matching the web's "Move to Done anyway". Alternative: refuse in the app and point to the web. open
  9. D9 Should one Feature's board be in the app, read-only, with stories by state and task counts? Recommended: yes. Alternative: no drill-in, just Open on web. open
  10. D10 Should short answers show in a sheet and long ones (the digest) open as a screen? Recommended: yes. Alternative: always a sheet. open
  11. D11 Should Read it out use on-device text to speech in v1? Recommended: yes. Alternative: answers are on screen only in v1. open
  12. D12 Should "since yesterday" in the banner mean since you last pressed Mark as seen? Recommended: since last seen. Alternative: a fixed 24 hours. open

Open questions

  • Health and the status line are new fields on a Feature. Should the web's Features board show them too?
  • Do Epics get a view in the app, or only a filter on this tab ("show SHIP-905")?
  • Should Needs you on a Feature include asks on its stories? The mock-up counts them (SHIP-914 on the board).
  • Change priority by voice works on backlog rank, but this list is grouped by state. Should a rank change show the new order in the preview?

Retro 8 states · retro.html · notes in retro.md

The current sprint's retro: dictate points, +1, turn a point into an action item, and settle what agents left to decide.

Decisions (Joel, 8 Nov 2026)

  1. D1 Should Notes show one category at a time, picked by four tiles, as on the mobile web (SHIP-727 D1)? Recommended: yes.
  2. D2 Should Notes open on the first category with points to decide? Recommended: yes, Went badly in the mock-up.
  3. D3 Should points get +1 (supporters stored per point)? This is new: the web has no voting Recommended: yes.
  4. D4 Should +1 only sort points within a theme and show faces, never deciding anything or feeding Recurring? Recommended: yes.
  5. D5 When no category is said, should the model pick one and the preview offer a one-tap change? Recommended: yes.
  6. D6 Should a dictated point need a start phrase such as "retro note:" or a category name? Recommended: yes, so commands and notes can't be confused.
  7. D7 Should an action item made by voice take the point's text word for word as its title? Recommended: yes, following the voice rule.
  8. D8 Should "that" mean the point you last touched (tapped or +1'd), highlighted? Recommended: yes, with the model asking when nothing was touched.
  9. D9 Can people +1 an agent's point? Recommended: yes.
  10. D10 Should theme merges proposed by agents be answerable in the app's To decide view? Recommended: yes.
  11. D11 Should a spoken point keep its audio clip next to the transcript? Recommended: yes, for 30 days.
  12. D12 Should the Retro tab have a Features view with recurring themes? Recommended: yes.

Open questions
