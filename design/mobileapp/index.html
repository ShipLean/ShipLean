Phone app mock-ups

The native app (epic SHIP-905) does less than the web app. Voice goes in and text comes out: notes, keeping an eye on progress, answering what people and agents wait on you for, and changing priority. 7 areas, 77 states, light and dark, at 390 × 844. Throwaway HTML on a small kit (app.css, app.js) over ../tokens.css; not the Expo app.

Tenet: speak in, read out (Joel, 9 Nov 2026)

Reading is faster than listening, and speaking is faster than typing. So the app is built for speaking going in and reading coming out:

  • Input is voice first: hold to talk is the main way to add notes, answer, approve and reorder. Taps and typing are the fallback.
  • Output is always on screen. Answers, previews, digests and Inbox items are text to read. The app does not narrate the screen: no Read aloud, no text to speech, no "go through my inbox" walk-through that reads each item out.
  • Why: a person reads a sheet in a few seconds, but hearing the same text takes far longer and can't be skimmed. Speaking a reply is quicker than typing one on a phone. Each direction uses the faster channel.
  • How to apply: don't propose narration, spoken answers or audio-first modes in new designs. If something feels like it needs reading aloud, make it shorter on screen instead. Voice notes keep their clip (S3) because they are input someone may need to check, not narration.

Shared decisions (Joel, 8 Nov 2026, Lavish review: all recommended)

  • S1: "Feature board" in the app is progress across all Features; one Feature's own board is a read-only drill-in.
  • S2: a fifth tab, Inbox, holds everything waiting on you (inbox.html, decisions D1–D13 answered 9 Nov 2026 in inbox.md).
  • S3: a voice note keeps its audio clip next to the transcript for 30 days, playable by project members.
  • S4: answers to spoken questions show on screen (a sheet when short, a screen when long). Amended 9 Nov 2026 by the tenet above: no Read aloud or text to speech.
  • S5: Health (On track / At risk / Blocked) is a new Feature field, set by people and agents, shown on the web too.
  • S6: Needs you on a Feature counts the questions and decisions on its stories, everywhere.
  • S7: the app uses native sizes (44px tap targets, 15px body); SHIP-805 stays the rule for the mobile web.
  • Agents never wait for an answer: anything asked of a person is answered on the work item, for the next agent that picks it up (Work item D7).

Projects and the tab bar (Joel, 9 Nov 2026, Lavish review: all recommended)

  • Projects is the root of the app. A project's tabs open on top of it. Every tab root (Inbox, Backlog, Features, Retro) has the project's key tile at the top left (App.tabRoot), with the project name as the subtitle. Tapping the tile, or swiping back, returns to Projects. Detail screens keep their back button.
  • Account is on Projects only (the avatar at the top right), together with Sign out and the way into Settings › Voice.
  • After setup: Use ShipLean while this downloads leads to Projects, or straight in when you have one project. A project you've never opened starts on Inbox. Later launches reopen the last project on the tab you left.
  • Switching keeps the tab you're on. "Switch to Payments" works by voice from any tab, with no preview (no data changes), only a toast with Back. A dot on the tile means something new waits on you in another project.

Inbox 16 states · inbox.html · notes in inbox.md

The first tab: everything waiting on you, longest waiting first, answered from the row by a tap, typing or voice.

Decisions

  1. D1. Should the Inbox show one row per work item, with all its asks inside? Recommended: yes, one row per item, showing the first ask and opening the rest in place. That keeps SHIP-907's 8 decisions to one row. Alternative: one row per ask.
  2. D2. What order should the Inbox use? Recommended: the Feature's backlog rank, with a Story's row under its Feature's row, so the most important work comes first. Alternative: longest waiting first.
  3. D3. Should a Story's question get its own row instead of being folded into its Feature? Recommended: its own row, indented under the Feature, because the answer is posted on the Story. The Feature's Needs you count still includes it (S6). Alternative: group every ask under a Feature heading.
  4. D4. Should tapping Approve on a single decision apply it at once? Recommended: yes, with Undo. A tap is explicit, while a voice approval still gets the preview (Work item D6). Alternative: every approval opens a preview.
  5. D5. Should Approve all always open the review sheet? Recommended: yes. Every decision is listed with its recommended answer chosen, and nothing is sent until Confirm N. Alternative: approve all with one tap and rely on Undo.
  6. D6. Should Reply open a sheet for that one question? Recommended: yes. The sheet has quick answers, typing and hold to talk, with the question as the context. Alternative: Reply opens the work item and focuses its dock.
  7. D7. When an ask is answered, should its row leave Waiting at once? Recommended: yes, into Answered, which shows what happened next (an agent picked it up, or the person replied). Alternative: keep the row greyed out until the next refresh.
  8. D8. What lands in the Inbox? Recommended: decisions and Needs input questions addressed to you, plus any on items you own that have no addressee. Alternative: also @mentions in notes, and "Still true?" for your stale status lines.
  9. D9. Should there be a way to snooze an item? Recommended: no snooze in v1, because hiding an ask hides work that waits on it. Alternative: Later, which hides an item for a day.
  10. D10. Should the app have a "Go through my inbox" walk-through? Recommended: yes. It reads each item aloud and takes spoken answers, one at a time, each previewed. Alternative: no walk-through in v1, only voice from the list.
  11. D11. What should the tab badge count? Recommended: items (4). Alternative: asks (13).
  12. D12. What happens to answers given offline? Recommended: the Inbox shows as last loaded, and taps and answers wait, marked "sends when you're online". Voice still works. Alternative: the Inbox is read-only offline.
  13. D13. Should a new item send a push notification? Recommended: yes, for asks addressed to you by name. Asks on items you own only update the badge. Alternative: badge only, no push in v1.

Install and setup 15 states · setup.html · notes in setup.md

First run: sign in, choose the voice models, download them while the app stays usable, and change them later in Settings.

Decisions

  1. D1. Can people skip voice entirely at first run? Recommended: yes, with "Skip voice for now" on the model screen. Nothing downloads, and voice is set up later from the greyed mic or Settings › Voice. Alternative: no skip, so every install downloads the models at first run, as SHIP-906 D3 reads literally.
  2. D2. Should Auto be selected for people, or should they have to pick? Recommended: Auto selected and marked Recommended, showing what it picked ("This phone: 12 GB → Qwen3 4B"), so one tap continues. Alternative: nothing selected until the person chooses a model.
  3. D3. Does sign-in come before the model choice? Recommended: sign in first (step 1), then models (step 2), so nobody downloads 3 GB for a server they can't reach. Alternative: models first, so the download starts before sign-in.
  4. D4. Should the download start before sign-in finishes? Recommended: no. It starts when the person taps Download and continue, and the microphone step and the rest of the app run while it downloads. Alternative: start the speech model (0.6 GB, the same on every phone) as soon as the app opens, and add the command model after the choice.
  5. D5. Can a phone with 8 GB pick Qwen3 4B? Recommended: yes, with a warning on the option (slow, may be closed when memory runs low). The app still checks free memory before loading, as on iOS. Alternative: 4B only at 11 GB reported or more, greyed out below that.
  6. D6. What happens on phones under 8 GB, such as the iPhone 15 (6 GB)? Recommended: voice is offered with Qwen3 1.7B only, and 4B is shown greyed with the reason; below 6 GB, setup skips voice and says why. Alternative: no voice on any phone under 8 GB, and setup skips the voice step with a line saying why.
  7. D7. Should Wi-Fi only be on by default? Recommended: on, asking each time the download would use mobile data, with the size left; "Use mobile data for this download" applies to that download only. Alternative: off by default, so the download uses whatever connection is there.
  8. D8. Does deleting the models keep the voice settings? Recommended: yes. The model choice and Wi-Fi only are kept, voice turns off, and the mic offers Set up voice, which downloads the same models again. Alternative: deleting resets everything, as if voice had been skipped.
  9. D9. Where does the model attribution live? Recommended: a Model licences row in Settings › Voice that names both licences, opening a page with the full attribution, and the same entries in the app's About › Licences. Alternative: only in the app's general About › Licences list.
  10. D10. When does the app ask for the microphone? Recommended: during setup, right after the model choice, with an explainer first. Alternative: the first time the person holds the mic.
  11. D11. When switching models, which goes first: the old one deleted or the new one downloaded? Recommended: download the new model and check it first, keep using the old one until then, and delete the old one after; the sheet says so. Alternative: delete the old model first to save space, leaving voice off until the new one is ready.
  12. D12. What memory figure does the app show? Recommended: the figure as sold, rounded up from what the phone reports (11.2 GB shows as 12 GB), with Auto's rule still at 11 GB reported. Alternative: show the reported figure ("11.2 GB").
  13. D13. Should there be a guided first command once voice is ready? Recommended: yes, once, a coach mark at the mic with "What needs me?", which is read-only and safe. Alternative: only a toast saying "Voice is ready".
  14. D14. Should the model screen show a time per command? Recommended: yes, an estimate by phone class ("About 5 s a command here"), replaced by numbers from the test set before release. Alternative: relative words only (Quicker, More accurate) with no numbers.

Open questions

  • If the 4B model passes the size check but fails the memory check when it loads, should voice fall back to 1.7B? That means downloading another 1.1 GB, or keeping 1.7B on the phone as a fallback.
  • If the microphone is refused, what does the mic do: open an explainer with "Open phone settings", or stay greyed with a label?
  • Do self-hosted servers download the models from our CDN, or does the ShipLean server host them, for instances with no internet access?
  • Should the download pause on low battery or in battery saver?
  • Should Delete models also be offered when the phone is low on space, for example from the system's storage warnings?
  • Is "Same on every phone" right for the speech model if a smaller one (Moonshine) becomes the fallback for the S22?
