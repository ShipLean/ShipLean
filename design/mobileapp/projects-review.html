Projects and switching project

Every tab in the phone app shows one project. SHIP-907 D7 makes Projects the first signed-in screen, but SHIP-916’s setup went straight into the tabs, and the tab bar had no project control. Decided 9 Nov 2026: Projects is the root of the app. A project’s tabs open on top of it, and the project’s key, shown as a tile at the top left of every tab root, takes you back.

Decisions (Joel, 9 Nov 2026: all recommended)

  1. D1 Where does switching project live?Projects is the root of the app. A project’s tabs open on top of it, and the tile (or a swipe back) returns to it.
  2. D2 What on a tab root shows and switches the project?The project’s key tile at the top left of every tab root, with the project name as the subtitle.
  3. D3 Where is Account?Only on Projects (the avatar at the top right, as in SHIP-907), with Sign out and the way into Settings › Voice.
  4. D4 What does the app open on after the first run?The last project, on the tab you left, with Projects one tap away.
  5. D5 What happens with exactly one project?Skip Projects after sign-in and setup and go straight in. The tile still opens Projects.
  6. D6 Which tab opens when you switch?The tab you’re on. A project you’ve never opened starts on Inbox.
  7. D7 Does the app show what waits on you in other projects?Yes: needs-you counts on Projects, and a dot on the tile when something new waits in another project.
  8. D8 Can you switch by voice?Yes: “open Payments” and “what needs me?” on Projects, “switch to <project>” from any tab. No preview; a toast with Back.
  9. D9 How is the Projects list ordered?Recent (the last 3 opened), then All alphabetical; the first time, one alphabetical list.
  10. D10 Can you create a project in the app?No. The empty state says how to get added and points to the web.
  11. D11 What happens when the project the app reopens is gone?Land on Projects with a notice saying which project and why.

Open questions

  • Is Inbox per project, as the mock-ups show, or should there also be an “All projects” Inbox?
  • When you’re in SHIP and say a key from another project (“approve PAY-31”), should the preview act there and label the change with its project, or offer to switch first?
  • Once push notifications and links exist, should opening an item in another project switch project silently, with the same toast?
  • Should a Projects row show more than the sprint line, such as health or the number of Features at risk?
