Checkout v2: design v0.2

Second round. v0.1's questions are answered inline; the big change is keeping card tokens in the vault.

1. Approach

Saved cards never touch our database. Checkout asks the vault for the customer's tokens and hands the chosen token to the payment provider, so the storefront stays out of PCI scope.

2. Conversion by step

StepToday (mobile)TargetOwner
Address84%88%Address autocomplete (SHIP-144)
Payment71%80%Saved cards (SHIP-141)
Paid43%50%Everything above

3. The saved-card picker

Radio rows rather than cards, so it still works with six or more saved cards. Each row shows what the vault returns and nothing else:
