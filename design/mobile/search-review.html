Retrospectives search on a phone

Search lists every point from every retro in the project, one row per point, under a search box, six filters, three checkboxes and Clear. Selecting a row shows its whole retro in a 380px panel on the right. At 900px and below this design keeps every filter, match and action. The search field gets its own row, and the filters move into one Filter sheet. Each one you set shows as a chip you can remove. Every point is a three-line row with the match marked. Tapping a row opens its whole retro full screen at that point, and ‹ › step through the results without going back.

Today: the desktop page in a 390px window

The real SPA (PR #69 at 3c4b0bc3) at 390 × 844 and 768 × 1024, using the page-shot fixtures and without SHIP-713's shell. Scroll inside each phone to see the whole page.

  1. The results can't be seen. The page is a two-column grid (results | a 380px retro). At 390px the retro takes the whole width and the results column is 0px wide (measured), so no row can be read or tapped. All you see is the first result's retro.
  2. The filters (search, six selects, three checkboxes, Clear) wrap onto seven rows, about 240px. The whole block is sticky, so it stays on screen over a third of the phone (rule 12).
  3. The search box and selects are 13px, so iOS Safari zooms in when you tap one (rule 14). The checkboxes are 13px targets (rule 13).
  4. The retro panel is sticky too, with its own scroll box (max-height: calc(100vh - 12rem)): a scroll area inside the page.
  5. On a 768px tablet the Point column is about 120px, so each point shows three words (“Could not take th…”). The full text, a root-cause match and the theme name are only in a hover title (rule 5).

Proposed acceptance criteria for SHIP-730

With the decisions as settled on 7 Nov. They're on SHIP-730.

  1. At 390 × 844, in both themes, Retrospectives › Search has no page-level sideways scroll with any filter, search, open retro, sheet or full-screen view. Above 900px nothing changes.
  2. The search field is alone on the first row, full width, with 16px text. Its keyboard key says Search and closes the keyboard; results follow typing as on desktop.
  3. Under it, one row: Filter, with the number of filters set, and “n points in m retros”. Nothing on the page sticks.
  4. Filter opens a bottom sheet with Category, Level, Written by and Decision as choice chips, Sprint and Theme as native selects, and No sprint, Unthemed and Recurring themes only as switches. Each change applies at once and is written to the address. The foot has Clear and “Show n points” with the live count.
  5. Each filter set shows as a chip under the bar; ✕ removes it. With two or more, Clear all is shown. Opening Search from Insights with a theme shows that theme's chip.
  6. Each point is one row: category and work item key; the point on up to two lines with the match marked; the root cause when it matched there, or “Match in the raw failure log”; the theme (↻ when recurring), sprint and author. Every row is at least 32px and tapping anywhere on it opens the retro.
  7. Tapping a row opens its whole retro full screen, scrolled to the point, which is outlined. The top bar has ✕ at the left, the retro's type, key and title with “Result n of m · sprint”, and ‹ › that step through the results in order (disabled at the ends).
  8. Opening a retro adds a history entry (selected, point), so Back closes it; stepping replaces the entry. Reloading with them in the address opens the same retro at the same point.
  9. In the open retro, + Action item and Not needed are at least 32px; the # theme opens the Move to theme sheet; ⋯ on a person's own point opens a sheet with Move to theme… and Archive; text fields are 16px.
  10. + Action item opens the shared full-screen New action item dialog from SHIP-727, seeded with the point.
  11. No results shows what was searched and a Clear search and filters button when anything is set.
  12. On a 768px tablet the layout is the phone's, rows at full width.
  13. Page screenshots at 390 × 844 in both themes sit next to the desktop ones: the results, the Filter sheet, the open retro and No results.
  14. Analytics: nothing new. The viewport stamped on retro.searched lets us compare searches per week on phones and on desktop, against the share of Search screen.viewed from phones, for four weeks before and after this ships.

Owned elsewhere

  • SHIP-805: the shared rules. Everything here fits them; D6 (b) would need a rule 1 change.
  • SHIP-713: the top bar, drawer (Retrospectives › Search), Sheet, scrim and the viewport property.
  • SHIP-727: the sheet, Move to theme, the note's ⋯ and the full-screen New action item dialog, built once in the shared retro components (its D8).
  • SHIP-714: the item opened full screen from an action item chip. SHIP-729: Open SHIP-101's retros.
  • SHIP-731: Insights' “All n in Search” links land here with ?theme=.

Not in this design

  • Matching and filtering don't change: the rows, counts and raw-log rule are the server's, as now.
  • No sort, saved searches or recent searches: desktop has none either.
  • The mock's desktop filters caught up with the real app (No sprint, Unthemed, Clear, Undecided / Actioned / Not needed, every filter in the address). Its desktop look is otherwise unchanged, and its page shots are regenerated.
