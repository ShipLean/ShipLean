SHIP-731 · Mobile retrospective insights · design for review

Retrospective insights on a phone

Insights has three views over every retro in the project: Recurring themes (a heat table of mentions per sprint or week, with status, action items and the last change), Action items (a 10-column table) and Changes & impact (a chart per closed action item). At 900px and below this design keeps every number and every action, and changes how they fit. Each theme becomes a card with a heat strip, and tapping it opens everything about the theme in one sheet. Action items become cards. Impact charts sit one a row, and you tap a bar to read it. The three views are equal tabs, Range and ⋯ share one row, and Merge themes opens full screen.

Today: the desktop page in a 390px window

The real SPA (main at 918cf067) at 390 × 844 and 768 × 1024, using the page-shot fixtures and without SHIP-713's shell. Scroll inside each phone to see the whole page.

  1. The view switch doesn't fit: “Recurring themes” wraps onto two lines, “Action items” is cut to “Acti…” and “Changes & impact” is off the screen, so the third view can't be reached.
  2. The themes table has 13 columns. At 390px you see the theme and four sprints; S24, No sprint, Total, Status, Action items and Last change are behind a sideways scroll inside the panel. The status is the point of the page, and it's off screen.
  3. Theme names wrap every word (“Shared- / branch / merge / conflicts”), so each row is about 70px tall.
  4. The Action items table has 10 columns. The title column wraps to five lines and Theme, Raised, State, Sprint, Pts, Owner and Impact need a sideways scroll.
  5. Status, Last change, No sprint and Unthemed explain themselves only in hover tooltips (rule 5). The ✎ rename button and the “+ Action item” buttons are about 20px.
  6. Changes & impact already fits: the charts and numbers read well at 390px. The bars' values show only on hover, and a long explanation sits above the first card.
  7. Merge themes… is a 620px dialog with 13px checkboxes and an 11px select, so iOS Safari zooms in (rule 14).

Proposed acceptance criteria for SHIP-731

With the decisions as settled on 7 Nov. They're on SHIP-731.

  1. At 390 × 844, in both themes, Retrospective insights has no page-level sideways scroll in any view, range (6, 3 and all sprints, 6 and 12 weeks), sheet or full-screen view. Above 900px nothing changes.
  2. The three views are one row of equal tabs (Themes | Action items | Impact) that all fit at 320px; the view is kept in the address as view.
  3. Under the tabs, one row: Range ▾ and ⋯. Range opens a bottom sheet with By sprint (Last 6, Last 3, All sprints) and By week (Last 6, Last 12 weeks); picking one applies it and closes. ⋯ holds Merge themes…, Give unthemed points themes (with the count), What the statuses and verdicts mean, Search retrospectives and Retrospectives by Feature. Nothing on the page sticks.
  4. Recurring themes shows one card per Went badly / Not in the plan theme in the desktop order: name, status chip, a heat strip with one cell per shown sprint or week (12 at 12 weeks), Total and its no-sprint count, the action item chips or “+ Action item”, and the last-change verdict. An axis line names the first and last column. Unthemed is last and links to Give them themes.
  5. Tapping a theme card opens a bottom sheet with what its status means, its count for every shown column with labels, no-sprint and total, its action items, “+ Action item” on its open points, the last change with its meaning and averages, the three latest mentions, All n in Search, Rename… and Merge into another theme….
  6. Rename… shows a 16px name field in the sheet with Discard and Save; Save renames the theme everywhere.
  7. No tooltip is hover-only: Status, Last change, No sprint and Unthemed are explained in a What these mean sheet, opened from a chip (that entry highlighted), from the section heading, from ⋯ and from How verdicts work.
  8. The proposed merge stacks its two themes and shows Keep apart and Merge as full-width buttons of at least 32px.
  9. Action items shows one card per item, open first then Done: key, state, points, title, theme (↻ when recurring), fix, covers, raised, sprint or Pull into the next sprint, owner and impact. Tapping a card opens the item full screen.
  10. Changes & impact shows one card a row. Tapping a bar marks it and reads out its column and count under the chart.
  11. Merge themes… is full screen with ✕ at the top left and Merge at the top right, 40px checkbox rows, a 16px Into select and the combined preview. Merge stays disabled until a source and a target are picked.
  12. “+ Action item” opens the shared full-screen New action item dialog from SHIP-727.
  13. On a 768px tablet the theme, action item and impact cards sit two a row.
  14. Page screenshots at 390 × 844 in both themes sit next to the desktop ones: retro-insights, retro-insights-weeks, retro-insights-theme, retro-insights-actions and retro-insights-impact.
  15. Analytics: nothing new. The viewport stamped on retro.themes_merged, retro.theme_renamed, retro.merge_kept_apart and retro.action_created lets us compare those per week on phones and on desktop, against the share of Insights screen.viewed from phones, for four weeks before and after this ships.
