SHIP-727 · Mobile sprint retrospective · design for review

The sprint Retro tab on a phone

The Retro tab puts every retrospective from the sprint on one page: four category columns of post-it notes, a rail with Coverage, Action items and Recurring themes, and a By work item view. At 900px and below this design keeps every note and every decision, and changes how they fit. Four category tiles pick the one category on screen. The four numbers open the rail's sections as bottom sheets. Filter and ⋯ More hold the rest of the toolbar. By work item collapses to one card per item. Notes, decisions, Add and the New action item dialog are still the page's own code, at touch sizes.

Today: the desktop page in a 390px window

The real SPA (main at 918cf067) at 390 × 844 and 768 × 1024, using the page-shot fixtures and without SHIP-713's shell. Scroll inside each phone to see the whole page.

  1. The rail is always 18rem wide (grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_18rem], no breakpoint), so at 390px Coverage, Action items and Recurring are drawn on top of the Feature retro and the Went well notes.
  2. The board gets about 150px: notes wrap every two or three words, and the agent chip is cut to “Claude Code · …”.
  3. The page is 3,300px long at 390px, and By work item is 6,900px, because each item repeats the four columns.
  4. The toolbar wraps onto three rows and is sticky top-0, which breaks SHIP-805 rule 12.
  5. The four numbers are pushed right, under the header. The Recurring chip and the numbers explain themselves only in hover tooltips (rule 5).
  6. “+ Action item”, “Not needed” and ⋯ are 18–24px tall. The Not needed and Add text boxes use 11px text, so iOS Safari zooms in (rule 14).
  7. On a 768px tablet the rail fits, but the board gets two narrow columns. The page has room for more.

Proposed acceptance criteria for SHIP-727

With the decisions as settled on 7 Nov. They're on SHIP-727.

  1. At 390 × 844, in both themes, the sprint Retro tab has no page-level sideways scroll in By category, in By work item, or with any sheet or the New action item dialog open. Only the sprint tabs scroll sideways, inside their own box. Above 900px nothing changes; the 18rem rail is still there.
  2. The sprint header and tabs match the other sprint tabs (SHIP-725 D10) and come from the same component.
  3. The four numbers are one row of buttons. Retros, Recurring themes and Action items open Coverage, Recurring and Action items as bottom sheets with the rail's content. Points to decide turns To decide on and off and shows pressed while it's on.
  4. The toolbar is one row: By category | By work item · Filter (n) · ⋯. Filter holds Written by and Points (All points | To decide), with Clear and Show points. ⋯ More holds the three rail sections, Retrospective insights, Search retrospectives and All sprints. Nothing on the page sticks.
  5. Four category tiles show each category's count and how many points are left to decide. Tapping a tile shows only that category, full width, grouped by theme. The tile is kept in the address as cat. Turning on To decide while Went well is showing moves to the first category with points to decide.
  6. On a note, “+ Action item”, “Not needed”, ⋯ and # theme are at least 32px. ⋯ (your own points only) opens Move to theme and Archive. # theme opens Move to theme as a sheet. The Recurring chip opens the Recurring sheet. No tooltip is hover-only.
  7. The Not needed reason, the Add text and root cause use 16px text. About is a native select. A point you add stays visible, as on desktop.
  8. New action item is full screen with ✕ at the top left and Create (or Add) at the top right. It behaves as the desktop dialog does.
  9. By work item shows one collapsed card per item with its four counts, “n to decide” and progress. Tapping a card opens it to show its notes by category (empty categories hidden), its raw failure log and a link to the item's retro page.
  10. On a 768px tablet the tiles sit four in a row and a category's notes fill two columns.
  11. Page screenshots at 390 × 844 in both themes sit next to the desktop ones: sprint-retro, sprint-retro-unplanned, sprint-retro-decide, sprint-retro-items, sprint-retro-action and sprint-retro-coverage.
  12. Analytics: nothing new. The viewport stamped on retro.point_not_needed, retro.action_created, retro.action_points_added and retro.point_added lets us compare points decided per week on phones and on desktop, against the share of Retro tab views from phones, before and after this ships.

Owned elsewhere

  • SHIP-805: the shared rules. Nothing here needs a rule changed. The tiles stay on screen without sideways scroll, so rule 7 is untouched.
  • SHIP-713: the top bar, drawer, Sheet, scrim and the viewport property.
  • SHIP-725 (and SHIP-724, SHIP-726): the sprint header and tabs (D10). This page copies that treatment; build it once for every sprint tab.
  • SHIP-728 to SHIP-731: the other retro pages use the same note (RetroNote), the same New action item dialog and the same theme picker (D8).

Not in this design

  • The item's own retro page (retro-item.html), which the cards' Open link goes to, belongs to SHIP-729.
  • Theme picker search and “New theme…” are drawn but not wired up: the desktop picker hasn't been designed beyond a toast either.
  • The New action item dialog's fields are unchanged; only its frame changes.
