Today at 390px

The built page (phone page shots, for orientation only; the design follows the rules and the desktop's content, not this).

  1. The days and note inputs sit on one line under each name, so the note is cut off at the right edge (R1a's finding: the day-off input is clipped).
  2. The days field is the only way to change days; a half day means typing on a numeric keyboard.
  3. Remove (×) is a 16px glyph beside the points, easy to hit by mistake and under the 32px target.
  4. Nothing on the row shows how much of the sprint a person is in; only the field's number.

Proposed

Each phone is one state of the mock; the chips under it name the decisions it shows. Everything scrolls with the page except the top bar.
