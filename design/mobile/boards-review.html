The three boards on a phone

One treatment for the Stories, Features and Epics boards at 900px and below. The board shows one column at a time in a sideways pager, cards move by press-and-hold drag (holding at the screen's edge turns the page) or through a ⋯ action sheet, and every popover becomes a bottom sheet. The phones below are the real reference screens (board.html, features-board.html) running live at 390 × 844 in ShipLean's own design system, so you can tap them: turn columns, press and hold a card and drag it, open ⋯ on a card and move it, filter, assign.

Today: a 1120px board in a 390px window

The reference boards at 390px with the touch layer switched off (?notouch), which is what the app does: BoardGrid.tsx:26 gives the grid min-w-[1120px], so the board scrolls sideways under the touch shell and you see about one and a half columns. The toolbar wraps to three rows, cards move only by HTML drag and drop (which touch screens don't fire) or Alt+arrow keys, and ⓘ and the assignee menu are anchored popovers.

How the board fits a phone

The pieces, top to bottom. Only the top bar is pinned (SHIP-805 rule 12); everything else scrolls with the page.

  1. Top bar from SHIP-713, unchanged: Menu, Boards › Stories, presence, Live, Search. The drawer already has the Epics · Features · Stories switch.
  2. Toolbar, one row (rule 8): the level switch, Filter (n), and ⋯ for Swimlanes or Lanes, Full or Compact cards, and Board settings.
  3. Meta line: 19 open · 86 pts · Swimlanes, and “Filtered: 1 of 26 cards” while a filter is on, so you can see what you're looking at once the controls have gone into ⋯.
  4. Column chips: one per column with its count against the WIP limit (red when over). The column on screen is highlighted; tapping one turns to it.
  5. The column: its header (name, WIP, state per type, ⓘ), then each lane's short header and its cards. A split column shows Doing, then Done. Lanes with no cards in this column are left out.
  6. The next column peeks 36px in from the right, so it's clear there's more. Swipe to turn; pages snap. On a tablet the same pager shows two and a bit columns.
  7. ⋯ on every card opens its actions: Open, Assign or Owner, what's under it, Move to (each column with its WIP), Lane, and Move to top or bottom. Press and hold lifts the card: drag it to a column or lane and let go; hold it at the left or right edge to turn the page; let go without moving and the actions open.

Proposed: 900px and below

Each phone opens a state through its URL; “Open” shows it full size (narrow the window below 900px). Press and hold a card (mouse or touch) to drag it; hold it at a phone's edge to turn the page. Moves, assigning and the Move to Done check go through the board's own code, so the state mapping, pull rule, WIP and toasts are the real ones.

Proposed acceptance criteria for SHIP-719

With the round 1 decisions. Ready to go on SHIP-719.

  1. At 390 × 844 the Stories, Features and Epics boards have no page-level sideways scroll, in both themes. Only the column pager and the column chips scroll sideways, each inside its own box.
  2. At 900px and below one column shows at a time with the next one peeking; swiping snaps to a column; the column chips show each column's count against its WIP limit (danger when over) and turn to that column when tapped. Above 900px nothing changes.
  3. Every card can be moved on a touch screen two ways, both behaving as the same drop on desktop (state per type, the pull rule, lane criteria, WIP over-limit warning, the Move to Done check): press and hold, then drag to a column or lane, holding at the screen edge to turn the page; or ⋯ → Move to a column, a lane, or the top or bottom of its column. Letting go of a held card without moving opens its actions; a swipe that starts on a card still scrolls.
  4. Swimlanes (Expedite, Default) and Lanes by Epic show inside each column with their own header and count; collapsing a lane collapses it in every column.
  5. Filter, ⋯ More, ⓘ, Assign/Owner, the card actions and the Move to Done check are bottom sheets built on SHIP-713's Sheet; the toolbar is one row (level, Filter (n), ⋯).
  6. Tapping a card opens the item modal full screen; ✕ comes back to the same column of the board.
  7. Page screenshots at 390 × 844 in both themes: board, board-ready (split column and lanes), board-card-actions, features-board, features-board-lanes, epics-board, next to the existing desktop ones; the reference screens in design/screens gain the layer shown here.
  8. Analytics: board.card_moved carries method (drag, touch_drag, keyboard, sheet) and the shared viewport property, so we can compare moves per board view, week by week, on phones against desktop, and before and after this ships.
