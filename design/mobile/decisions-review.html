What changes on a phone

  • Section chip: “Decisions 3” leads the SHIP-714 section chips; the number is what's waiting (M4).
  • Rows: number on the left, then the question, answer, state and who. The actions move under the row as 32px buttons: ✓ Confirm, Change, Discuss, ⋯ (SHIP-805 rules 5 and 13; M2).
  • ⋯ opens an action sheet titled with the decision (Withdraw, Copy link), over the shell's scrim (rule 8).
  • Change, Reply, Discuss open inline under the row, as on desktop (UI5, M1). Their inputs are 16px (rule 14); options stack, with the tag under the text.
  • Header: “Waiting on …” wraps under the title; ✓ Confirm N recommended sits on its own line (M3).
  • Long questions show in full (M5). The group hints (“in number order…”) are hidden.
  • Feature roll-up: key and title on one line, owner and counts under it.
  • History (UI10) moves below the page with the rest of the rail and shows the newest three lines, then Show all, as in SHIP-714 (M6).
  • Nothing is hover-only; nothing new is pinned (rule 12).
