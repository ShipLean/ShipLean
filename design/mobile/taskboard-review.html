SHIP-725 · Mobile sprint taskboard · design for review

The sprint Taskboard on a phone

The sprint Taskboard is the same grid as the Feature board: a row per story or bug, a column per task state, tasks as cards. So at 900px and below it gets the layout you settled for SHIP-722: one task state a page in a sideways pager, every story a lane on every page, ⋯ and long-press drag to move tasks, closed stories one line each, as on desktop. This page covers what only the Taskboard has: the sprint header and picker, the sprint tabs, the Unparented lane, Pick up, “All tasks done”, Close sprint and the Taskboard settings dialog. The phones below are the reference screen (taskboard.html) running live at 390 × 844 in ShipLean's own design system, so you can tap them.

Today: the desktop grid in a 390px window

The real SPA at 390 × 844 with the page-shot fixtures, before SHIP-713's shell. Scroll inside each phone to see the whole page.

  1. Only the Story / bug column and part of New fit (268px + 176px per state). Active, In review and Closed are off to the right.
  2. Tasks move only by HTML drag and drop, which touch screens don't fire. Pick up only shows on hover, which a phone doesn't have.
  3. The toolbar wraps onto three lines: “Group by” on two lines, faces, Settings, and “+ Add story or bug” cut off at the right.
  4. “All sprints” and “Close sprint” take a line of their own under the sprint name.
  5. Taskboard settings keeps its two-column dialog: the section list takes half the width and the rules are cut off at the right.

Proposed: 900px and below

Each phone opens a state through its URL; “Open” shows it full size (narrow the window below 900px). Swipe the pager or tap a state chip. Moves go through the reference board's own move, so the toasts and auto-assign are the real rules. Use the Preview panel's “Teammate's move” (in “Open”) to see the chip pulse.

Proposed acceptance criteria for SHIP-725

With the recommended decisions, on top of SHIP-722's. These go on SHIP-725 once you've settled them.

  1. At 390 × 844 the sprint Taskboard has no page-level sideways scroll, in both themes, grouped by Stories and by People. Only the sprint tabs, the state chips and the state pager scroll sideways, each in its own box. Above 900px nothing changes.
  2. The board meets SHIP-722's criteria for the pager, lanes, “None”, closed stories on one line, ⋯ and long-press moves, tap to open, Filter and the one-row toolbar, using the same shared Taskboard grid code.
  3. Unparented is the last lane on every page; a task can be dragged or moved (“No parent”) into it and added to it with + Task.
  4. An unassigned task in a New-category state shows Pick up without hover; Pick up (on the card or in the ⋯ sheet) assigns you and moves it to the first In progress state.
  5. When every task of an open story is in a Completed state, its lane offers Move to Resolved and ✕ on every page; nothing moves by itself.
  6. The Taskboard uses SHIP-724's shared phone sprint header (picker sheet, Current, ⋯ with Edit goal, Edit sprint, All sprints and Close sprint, the goal, the tabs); the board's ⋯ has Collapse all, Expand all and Taskboard settings.
  7. Taskboard settings opens full screen with ✕ at the top left, section chips, every field and swatch at 32px or more, inputs at 16px, and the SHIP-713 Save bar (Discard, Save).
  8. A teammate's move flashes the card, shows the toast and pulses the target state's chip without turning the page.
  9. Page screenshots at 390 × 844 in both themes next to the desktop ones: taskboard, taskboard-people, taskboard-task-actions, taskboard-settings.
  10. Analytics: work_item.moved from Taskboard.Move carries method (drag, touch drag, keyboard, sheet, pick up), shared with SHIP-722, next to viewport. We compare task moves per Taskboard view, week by week, on phones against desktop, before and after this ships.
